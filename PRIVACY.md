<h1 align="center">Privacy Policy</h1>

<p align="center"><em>Easy Password Generator · Last updated: 2026-10-12</em></p>

---

Easy Password Generator is **privacy-first and offline-only**. It does not
collect, store, or transmit any personal data or passwords. Everything happens
locally in your browser.

## What we store

Only your **generator preferences** are saved, using `chrome.storage.local` on
your own device:

- Password length
- Which character types are enabled (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols)
- Whether ambiguous characters are excluded

This never leaves your device and is never synced or uploaded.

## What we never do

- ❌ Store, log, or keep a history of generated passwords
- ❌ Read, inspect, or transmit anything you have typed into a page
- ❌ Collect personal or identifying information
- ❌ Use analytics, telemetry, advertising, or trackers
- ❌ Make network requests or load remote code
- ❌ Sell or share data with third parties

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| `contextMenus` | Add the “Generate Password” right-click item. |
| `storage` | Remember your generator preferences only. |
| `clipboardWrite` | Let the “Copy” button put a password on your clipboard. |
| Host access | Let the generator open on the field you right-click. It only writes the password into the field you choose — it never reads page content. |
